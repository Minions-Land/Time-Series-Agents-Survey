# Formal ledger evidence gap

This report audits **130** current paper cards against the evidence policy. The ledger contains 5 paper-verified cards and 125 survey-coded cards. Survey-coded records remain useful for corpus organization, but their claim and module boundaries are not described as fully verified findings.

Each promotion to `paper_verified` requires a stable original-paper locator, an exact quotation or source artifact, and a review of the LLM/GH/GTS/TSK boundary. Scholar status is a separate gate.
