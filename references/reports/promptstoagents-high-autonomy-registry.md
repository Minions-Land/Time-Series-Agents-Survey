# High-autonomy candidate registry from *From Prompts to Agents*

This registry preserves all 40 records coded L3/L4 in the companion corpus. The records are candidate discovery evidence, not an inclusion guarantee. Thirty-seven records were checked against the first two pages of the available arXiv PDF in this pass; six are source-reviewed exclusions, and 31 remain inclusion candidates pending production-Bib identity, full-paper classification, and Google Scholar receipt verification. The remaining records stay pending original-paper review.

`paper_claim_summary`, `harness_scope_assessment`, and `task_axis_assessment` are preliminary source-locatable coding fields. They do not replace the maintained work-level ledger until the work has a stable identity, a complete original-paper read, and a verified citation receipt.
