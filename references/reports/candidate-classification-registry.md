# Candidate classification registry

This table is the working two-axis registry for source-reviewed candidates. The Harness assessment describes whether the contribution is general, time-series-general, or task-specific; the task axis records where that mechanism is instantiated. Records remain outside the formal ledger until their production identity and Google Scholar Cite -> BibTeX receipt are complete. A Scholar-verified receipt is never inferred from arXiv, DOI, publisher, or comparison-survey metadata.
