# Chen et al. missing-system registry

This registry contains the 31 bibliographic records represented by the 30 Chen et al. Table 3 systems that were absent from the production bibliography when the coverage audit was built. ElliottAgents has two cited underlying works, so it occupies two rows.

The identity fields are copied from the official source snapshot of Chen et al.'s bibliography and are retained as discovery evidence. `source_survey_identity` does not mean that the original paper has been read, that the work belongs in the contribution corpus, or that its BibTeX has been verified through Google Scholar. Each record remains `needs_original_paper_review` and `pending` until the original paper and a matching Scholar Cite → BibTeX receipt are independently checked.

The `source_locator` column points to Chen et al.'s Appendix Table 1 and its problem grouping. The maintained contribution ledger is updated only after original-paper evidence establishes an Agent/Harness contribution and the work is assigned the two-axis taxonomy used by this survey.
