# RSI-derived editable figures used in the paper

The five-page editable deck `TimeSeriesAgent_Figures_RSI_template_working_copy_grouped.pptx` is the canonical grouped source. It retains native PowerPoint groups (`p:grpSp`) so every panel can be edited or ungrouped.

The paper now uses direct single-page PDF/SVG exports from these deck pages. Each page also has a one-slide grouped PPTX whose rendered pixels match the PDF export.

| Paper figure | Source deck page | PDF/SVG used by `main.tex` | One-slide editable PPT | Role |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| Figure 2 | 2 | `Figure2_organization_rsi_adapted.pdf/.svg` | `Figure2_organization_rsi_adapted_grouped.pptx` | survey organization |
| Figure 3 | 3 | `Figure3_construction_rsi_adapted.pdf/.svg` | `Figure3_construction_rsi_adapted_grouped.pptx` | contribution layers and Paper Card |
| Figure 6 | 5 | `Figure6_task_contracts_rsi_adapted.pdf/.svg` | `Figure6_task_contracts_rsi_adapted_grouped.pptx` | four task contracts |

The remaining paper figures keep their existing native grouped PPT/PDF pairs because they are separate section-specific visualizations. The old 16:9 versions remain in the repository as historical native redraws; they are no longer the active sources for Figures 2, 3, and 6.
