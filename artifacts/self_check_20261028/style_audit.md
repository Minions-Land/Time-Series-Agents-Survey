# Style self-check against Transformers in Time Series: A Survey

## Findings

The manuscript already shares the reference survey's strongest structural choices: it introduces a taxonomy before the detailed review, keeps the section order aligned with the taxonomy, places equations next to the mechanisms they explain, and closes technical groups with design trade-offs. The main weaknesses were concentrated in the front matter and in the amount of repeated framing.

1. The abstract described Paper Cards and the repository as if they were a primary contribution. The main contribution is the construction taxonomy; the structured records should support inspection and extension after that claim is established.
2. The opening of the Introduction repeated the same construction question across several paragraphs before stating the survey's organizing answer.
3. The Introduction did not state the reading route in one compact passage. The reference survey gives the reader a taxonomy and then follows it directly.
4. The technical sections already use a useful method pattern: mechanism, reason for the design, evidence, and comparison. That pattern should be preserved rather than adding more paper-by-paper summaries.
5. The conclusion already has the right one-paragraph form. It should remain concise and center the taxonomy, construction guidance, evaluation connection, and future directions.

## Revision decisions

- Rewrite the abstract around field importance, construction gap, taxonomy, comparative synthesis, evaluation guidance, and future directions. Mention the structured records only as a supporting resource.
- Rewrite the first Introduction paragraphs so the construction problem is stated once and the general-to-specific taxonomy follows as its answer.
- Add one compact roadmap sentence to the contribution section.
- Preserve the existing evidence-linked method paragraphs, formulas, figures, tables, and subsection conclusions.
- Keep Paper Cards and repository details in the Introduction contribution paragraph and Appendix, where they support the taxonomy rather than compete with it.
