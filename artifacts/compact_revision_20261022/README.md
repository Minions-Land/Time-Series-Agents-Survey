# Compact survey figures

Figures 4, 7, and 9–13 are recomposed to match the line-icon vocabulary of Figures 5 and 6. Richness comes from local analytical examples and module relationships, not oversized icons or type.

Each file in `pptx/` contains one slide with nested component groups. Text, connectors, analytical curves, and tables are editable; extracted line-icon interiors remain raster artwork. PDFs in `pdf/` are the manuscript exports. Captions are maintained in `main.tex`.

Generated source compositions and prompts are retained per figure. The final editable figures correct their scientific relationships and labels against the survey and source papers; generated drafts are not the authoritative scientific record.
